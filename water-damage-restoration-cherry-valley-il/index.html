Water damage restoration in Cherry Valley, IL is available through the 24/7 line. The village is about 14 miles southeast of Machesney Park by road, reached through Rockford, and uses ZIP code 61016.

Cherry Valley lies in Winnebago County and borders the southeast side of Rockford. Crews cover pumping, drying and cleanup for homes and businesses.

The Kishwaukee River runs through town.

The Kishwaukee River flows through Cherry Valley, and its north branch runs through the central business district. The village core sits just north of where the north and south branches meet.

Homes near the river face higher flood risk. See flood damage cleanup for river water cleanup and crawl space water damage for water below floors.

An older core and newer streets.

The village core has late-19th-century houses around its main street. The 2020 Census counted 1,338 housing units with 5.2 percent vacant. Older houses may have original plumbing and plaster walls.

Drying them requires care. The structural drying page explains moisture readings and equipment.

Landmarks and businesses in Cherry Valley.

Baumann Park holds Cherry Valley Lake, and CherryVale Mall is one of the first enclosed malls in Illinois. Kegel Motorcycle Company and Six Flags Hurricane Harbor Rockford are also local landmarks.

Businesses face the same risks as homes. See commercial water damage restoration for how those jobs work.

Getting to Cherry Valley.

The village is served by I-90, I-39, US 20 and US 51. Crews reach it from Machesney Park through Rockford, so the trip is longer than for closer towns.

The dispatcher will give a realistic expectation when you call. No arrival time is guaranteed.

Schools and neighbors.

Most of Cherry Valley is in Rockford School District 205, with a small Boone County portion in Belvidere Consolidated Unit School District 100. About 10 percent of the village area is in Boone County.

See the Rockford page for the larger city next door.

What to expect when you call from Cherry Valley.

The dispatcher will ask for your address, the source of the water and which rooms are wet. They will explain what to do before the crew arrives, including how to stop the source, protect electrics and move belongings.

Because Cherry Valley is farther from Machesney Park than the closest towns, the dispatcher will give a realistic expectation of timing. No arrival time is guaranteed, and same-day response is offered when available.

Water problems that fit Cherry Valley homes.

Homes in the village core are older, with late-19th-century houses around the main street, and older houses can have basements and original plumbing. Newer homes elsewhere in the village face appliance and supply-line failures.

River-adjacent properties near the Kishwaukee deserve extra attention during heavy rain and snowmelt. A working sump pump and clear drainage help. If water does enter, the sewage backup page explains why contaminated water is handled differently.

Insurance in Cherry Valley.

Sudden pipe and appliance losses are often covered. Flood and slow leaks often are not. Flood insurance is a separate policy.

Photograph the damage and keep invoices. The crew provides moisture records.
