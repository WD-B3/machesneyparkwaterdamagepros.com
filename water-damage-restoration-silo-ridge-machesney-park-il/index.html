Basements and finished lower levels.

A finished basement can hide a wet slab, wet framing and damp insulation behind a clean surface. When water gets in, carpet pad and lower drywall are often the first materials to need removal. The rest can often be dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Water heaters, laundry and supply lines.

Tank water heaters, washing machine hoses and sink supply lines are common sources of water damage in any home. See water heater leak cleanup for what to do when a tank fails and appliance leak water damage for hose and valve failures.

A water heater that is more than a decade old, a corroded fitting or a damp spot near a laundry hookup are worth fixing before they fail.

  • Check the base of the water heater for rust or dampness.
  • Inspect washing machine hoses for bulges or cracks.
  • Test your sump pump if you have one.
  • Fix a dripping fixture before it stains the cabinet.

Storm and roof leak water.

Insurance and documentation.

Homeowners insurance generally covers sudden water damage from a failed pipe or appliance and generally excludes flood and some drain or sump backups unless an endorsement was added. Ask your insurer before you decide anything, since the policy decides.

Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope of work help your insurer understand the loss. They do not guarantee payment.

Before the crew arrives in Silo Ridge.

These steps are general and aim to keep people safe first. If anything feels unsafe, stay out and call.

1Stop the source if it is safe.

Close the nearest shutoff valve or the main water valve. If the water came from outside, do not wade into it.

2Keep clear of electrics.

Do not touch outlets, cords or the panel if water is near them. Ask your utility or an electrician about power before going back in.

3Photograph the damage.

Take photos and short videos of the water, the affected rooms and damaged belongings once it is safe to enter.

4Lift what you safely can.

Move small items, rugs and paperwork to dry ground. Leave heavy furniture and anything you cannot lift safely.
