Every service below is handled from Machesney Park, IL by the same 24/7 dispatch line. The pages are grouped by cause and by material so you can find the one that fits your loss. If you are not sure which applies, start with water extraction for standing water or structural drying for damp walls and floors.

For the main overview, see the home page. To check whether your town is covered, see the service area page.

The groups below follow how losses actually start. Pipe and plumbing failures are the most common winter calls. Appliance and air conditioning leaks are common year round. Storms, floods and basement water follow heavy rain and snowmelt. Water removal and drying pages explain the work itself, and the floor and wall pages explain what happens to each material.

Every page names its own symptoms, steps and cost factors, and none of them promises a price or an arrival time. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and the 24/7 line answers at any hour if you would rather talk it through.

All water damage services.

Pipes and plumbing.

  • Burst Pipe Water DamagePressurized supply lines that fail and flood walls, ceilings and floors.
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamageIce-blocked or split lines that release water when they thaw.
  • Water Heater Leak CleanupTank failures, leaking fittings and relief valve discharge.
  • Slab Leak Water DamageLeaks in supply lines under a concrete slab that wet floors and subfloors.

Appliances and HVAC.

  • Appliance Leak Water DamageWashers, dishwashers, refrigerators and other appliances that leak.
  • AC Leak Water DamageCondensate lines, drain pans and air handlers that drip into ceilings and floors.
  • Kitchen Water DamageCabinets, sink bases and flooring after leaks under the sink or around fixtures.

Weather and building envelope.

  • Ceiling Water Damage RepairCeiling stains, sagging and collapse from roofs, upstairs plumbing and HVAC.
  • Storm Water DamageWind, rain and hail damage that lets water into the building.
  • Flood Damage CleanupRiver, groundwater and surface flooding, including contaminated water.
  • Basement Water DamageSump failures, seepage and flooding in below-grade rooms.
  • Crawl Space Water DamageStanding water, damp insulation and wood damage beneath the house.

Water removal and drying.

  • Water ExtractionStanding water pumped and extracted before it reaches subfloors and walls.
  • Structural DryingMoisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers that dry walls, floors and framing.
  • Sewage Backup CleanupToilets, tubs and floor drains that back up with contaminated water.

Floors and walls.

  • Hardwood Floor Water DamageCupping, crowning and buckling in wood and laminate floors after water.
  • Carpet Water DamageWet carpet and pad dried in place or removed depending on the water category.
  • Wall and Drywall Water DamageStained, soft or bubbling drywall and baseboards after water reaches a wall.

Commercial.

  • Commercial Water DamageOffices, shops and multi-unit buildings that need fast water recovery.

Secondary service.

  • Mold RemediationContainment and removal of mold after moisture problems, following S520 practice.
