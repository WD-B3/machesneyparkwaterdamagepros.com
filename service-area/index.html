Service Area Around Machesney Park, IL.

One 24/7 line covers Machesney Park and the nearby Winnebago County towns below.

☎ Call (815) 314-9281.

The home base is 1001 West Lane Road in Machesney Park, ZIP 61115. From there, the crew covers the towns below. Each page lists its ZIP codes and local water risks.

If your address is near a town border or you do not see your place listed, call and the dispatcher will confirm coverage.

Towns we serve.

Loves Park.

Four ZIP codes, a city split by two counties, and plenty of mid-century homes.

Roscoe.

A village that doubled its housing since 2000, along the Rock River.

Rockton.

A historic river village with older homes and the Macktown story.

Rockford.

A larger city with creek flooding history and several ZIP codes.

Cherry Valley.

A small village in the Kishwaukee River valley southeast of Rockford.

Neighborhoods in Machesney Park.

Shore Drive Area.

Near the Rock River, north of Harlem Road, where high water has reached homes before.

River Key.

A Rock River subdivision with streets named for the Keys and homes built in phases.

Timberlyne Hollow.

A community off Mitchell Road with attached and single-family homes and a village park.

Silo Ridge.

Off Vaughndale Drive and Rolling Hills Lane, next to a village neighborhood park.

ZIP codes by town.

Loves Park uses 61111, 61130, 61131 and 61132. Roscoe uses 61073. Rockford is covered across 61101 through 61106. Rockton uses 61072 and Cherry Valley uses 61016. Machesney Park itself is 61115.

Small places nearby, including South Beloit (61080), Winnebago (61088), Poplar Grove (61065) and Shirland, can call the same line.

Why the Rock River matters across the area.

The Rock River runs through or beside most of these communities, and the Machesney Park 2040 Comprehensive Plan reports that a significant portion of the village is in the 100-year floodplain. River flooding is treated as contaminated water and follows stricter cleanup steps.

See flood damage cleanup and basement water damage cleanup for how those losses are handled.

How coverage is confirmed.

Coverage is confirmed by address. When you call, the dispatcher asks for your street address, town and ZIP, then confirms whether the crew serves it. Places near a border, such as the east side of Rockford or the Boone County edge of Loves Park and Cherry Valley, are checked one by one.

If your address is outside the area, the dispatcher will say so plainly rather than promise help that may not arrive.

Distance and timing from Machesney Park.

Loves Park is the closest town, roughly four miles west by road. Roscoe is roughly seven miles to the northeast, Rockford about eight miles to the southwest and Rockton about eight miles north. Cherry Valley is farthest, about 14 miles to the southeast and reached through Rockford.

These are approximate road distances. Traffic, weather and call volume change real travel time, so no arrival time is guaranteed.

Which counties and school districts overlap.

Most of the area sits in Winnebago County. Small parts of Loves Park and Cherry Valley reach into Boone County. Several school districts overlap, including Harlem District 122, Rockford District 205, Kinnikinnick District 131 and Rockton District 140.

School and county lines do not change the service. They matter mainly when you need to know which village office or permit authority handles repairs at your address.

What water damage looks like across the area.

The communities share the Rock River valley, so river and groundwater flooding are possible in low areas, and spring snowmelt and heavy rain test basements everywhere. Older neighborhoods bring aging plumbing and water heaters, while newer subdivisions bring appliance hoses and supply lines that fail.

Winter adds frozen pipes and ice dams across the whole area. The local pages explain which problems matter most in each town.

If your town is not listed.

Small places around the listed towns, including South Beloit, Winnebago, Poplar Grove and Shirland, can call the same line. The dispatcher will confirm coverage from your address.

If you are unsure whether you are inside the area, call anyway. A short conversation answers it faster than reading maps.

Preparing before you call.

Have your street address, nearest cross street and ZIP ready. Know where your main water valve is. Note which rooms are wet and whether the water is clean, from a drain or from outside. If it is safe, take a few photos before anything is moved.

None of that is required to call. If you only know that water is coming through a ceiling, say so and the dispatcher will take it from there.

Questions about your address.

Machesney Park, Loves Park and Roscoe share borders and school districts, so a mailing town name does not always match the jurisdiction. The dispatcher goes by the street address, not the mailing name.

If you are unsure which village or county office handles permits at your address, ask the dispatcher or the local building office.

Services available everywhere in the area.

The same services apply across the area, from water extraction to commercial restoration and mold remediation. See the full services list to choose.

No arrival time is guaranteed. Same-day response is offered when available.
