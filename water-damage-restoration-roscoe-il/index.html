Newer homes, different water problems.

Newer homes have their own failure points. Supply lines to toilets and sinks, ice maker lines, washing machine hoses and water heater connections are common failure points. See appliance leak water damage for those causes.

Where Roscoe water damage starts.

Roscoe and the Rock River.

The village lies along the Rock River, and the historic Macktown area marks where earlier settlers built near the water. The bridge between Macktown and Roscoe washed away in 1851, which is a reminder that the river has shaped this area for a long time.

If river water enters a home, it is treated as contaminated. See flood damage cleanup for how that cleanup works and sewage backup cleanup for drain backups.

Storms and wind in Roscoe.

A May 22, 2011 EF-1 tornado hit the Harlem-Roscoe Fire Department Station Three on Willowbrook Road. It was about a mile long and 50 yards wide, and no injuries were reported.

Severe weather can pull shingles, break windows and drive rain into attics. The storm water damage page explains temporary protection and cleanup.

Insurance questions in Roscoe.

Sudden pipe, appliance and storm losses are often covered by homeowners insurance. Slow leaks, wear and flood generally are not. Flood coverage is a separate policy.

Photograph the damage and keep any failed parts. The crew provides moisture records for your claim.

Quick steps while you wait in Roscoe.
