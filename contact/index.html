What to have ready when you call.

Having a few facts ready speeds things up.

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from, if you know.
  • Which rooms and materials are wet.
  • Whether the water is clean, from a drain or from outside.
  • Your insurance company name, if you have it.
