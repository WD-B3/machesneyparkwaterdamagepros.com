What water damage looks like in Loves Park homes.

Common sources of water damage in Loves Park homes include four things. A basement floor drain can back up after a hard rain. A water heater can fail in a utility closet. A supply line under a kitchen sink can split overnight. A roof leak can stain a ceiling after snow melts.

Each of those starts with finding and stopping the source, then removing standing water and drying what remains. The water extraction page explains pumping and the structural drying page explains how wet framing and drywall are dried.

Insurance in Loves Park.

Standard homeowners insurance generally covers sudden water damage from a burst pipe or a failed appliance, and generally excludes flood and some drain or sump backups unless an endorsement was added. Flood insurance is a separate policy.

Photograph the damage before cleanup and keep your invoices. The crew documents moisture readings so your insurer has the facts it needs.

Before the crew arrives in Loves Park.

A few minutes of preparation before the crew arrives can limit the damage. These steps are general and safe for most homes.

1Stop the source.

Close the nearest shutoff valve or the main water valve. If the water came from outside, stay out of deep water.

2Protect electrics.

Do not touch outlets or the panel if water is near them. Cut power only from a dry spot.

3Lift valuables.

Move papers, electronics and small furniture to dry, higher surfaces.

4Photograph everything.

Take photos and video of the damage and the source before cleanup starts.
