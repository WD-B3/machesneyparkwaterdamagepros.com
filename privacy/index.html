Privacy Policy.

A plain description of what this site and our phone line collect.

☎ Call (815) 314-9281.

This policy explains what Machesney Park Water Damage Pros collects when you visit this website or call our line. We keep it short because we collect little.

Effective date: November 10, 2026.

Phone calls.

This site uses phone calls only and has no contact forms. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and the details you give us about the water damage. We use that to arrange service and follow up.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Website visits.

Our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. This data is used to keep the site running and secure.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows an embedded Google map, which may involve your browser contacting Google.

How we use and share information.

We use information to respond to your request, arrange service and improve how we operate. We share details with the restoration professionals who handle your job so that they can help you.

We do not sell personal information.

Your choices and contact.

You can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@machesneyparkwaterdamagepros.com or calling (815) 314-9281.

We may update this policy, and the effective date above will change when we do.
